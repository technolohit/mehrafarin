Mehrafarin Kolahdoozan offers the following forms of professional support.

## Services

### Individual psychotherapy
Regular sessions focused on emotional experience, relationships, and patterns that repeat in everyday life.

### Analytical psychotherapy
Closer attention to meaning, inner history, and what emerges in the therapeutic relationship, within an analytical orientation and psychoanalytic, psychodynamic, and Lacanian traditions.

### Psychological consultation
A professional conversation to clarify the present situation, needs, and a suitable next step.

### Online sessions
Sessions take place online.

## Who it is for

Services are for adults and couples.

## Language

Sessions are conducted in Persian (Farsi).
