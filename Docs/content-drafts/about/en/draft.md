Mehrafarin Kolahdoozan is a clinical psychologist and psychotherapist with an analytical orientation. She holds Psychology Organization License 22018.

## Professional orientation

Her work is grounded in an analytical orientation, with reference to psychoanalytic, psychodynamic, and Lacanian traditions.

Sessions are calm and careful, with attention to inner experience, relationships, and what tends to repeat in conversation.

## Who she works with

She works with adults and couples.

## Format and language of sessions

Sessions are online and conducted in Persian (Farsi).
