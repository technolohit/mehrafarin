Starting can be simple. The usual path looks like this.

## 1. Initial contact

Write by email or Telegram. Channel details are on the [Contact](/en/contact) page.

A first message only needs your name, a preferred way to reply, and a few lines about why you are writing. A detailed clinical history is not required.

## 2. First conversation

The first conversation is a chance to talk about where you are now and whether continuing feels like a good fit.

## 3. Ongoing online sessions

If you decide to continue, work proceeds in regular online sessions. A typical session lasts about 45 minutes. Therapy is conducted in Persian (Farsi).

## Fees

Session fees are not published on this website.
