# Voice

Brand voice for Mehrafarin Kolahdoozan public content (FA and EN).

Derived from repository build brief and existing Home tone. Not a license to invent professional facts.

---

## Positioning

- Calm, warm, premium, editorial, human, trustworthy, private, sophisticated, emotionally safe
- Professional portfolio for an independent psychotherapist — not hospital, clinic SaaS, marketplace, or aggressive funnel

## Do

- Prefer clarity and restraint over persuasion
- Use verified titles and license only when asserting credentials
- Allow silence where facts are UNKNOWN
- Keep Persian and English **native** quality (not literal mirror translations)
- State limits honestly (e.g. anti-guarantee note aligned with [SAFE-001])

## Don’t

- Hype, urgency tactics, miracle language, or outcome guarantees ([SAFE-001])
- Invent biography, specialties, testimonials, statistics, or clinic branding
- Sound like generic AI wellness marketing
- Overuse buzzwords (“holistic journey”, “transform your life”, etc.)

## Register by locale

| Locale | Notes |
|--------|--------|
| `fa` | RTL; respectful professional Persian; avoid stiff calques from English |
| `en` | Clear professional English; analytical without jargon walls |
