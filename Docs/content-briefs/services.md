# Content brief — Services

## Page purpose
Clear list of verified services for a simple portfolio site.

## Primary user intent
See what is offered and whether online Persian work fits.

## Audience
Adults and couples considering starting psychotherapy or consultation.

## Key questions answered
- Which services exist?
- Online or in person?
- For whom?
- Therapy language?

## Required Fact IDs
SVC-001, SVC-002, SVC-003, SVC-004, PRO-010, PRO-011, PRO-012, PRO-005, PRO-009 (context)

## Permitted claims
The four verified services; adults/couples as clients; online-only; Persian therapy; calm non-specialized descriptions.

## Prohibited claims
SAFE-001, SAFE-003, SAFE-008, SAFE-009; condition landers; invented extra services.

## Content structure
1. Intro (portfolio-simple)  
2. Service list (4 items, short)  
3. Who it’s for  
4. Format & language note  
5. Link to Process + Contact  

## CTA
Primary: Contact · Secondary: Process

## Internal links
→ Process, Contact, About · ← Home

## SEO / GEO
Service entity list consistency; no specialty keyword farms.

## Bilingual parity
Same four services; EN clarifies Persian therapy + online-only.

## Acceptance
Simple editorial list—not SaaS cards or diagnosis menus.
