# Content brief — Process

## Page purpose
Reduce uncertainty about how online work begins—concise, portfolio-oriented. No fees. No cancellation policy.

## Primary user intent
Understand first steps before contacting.

## Audience
Prospective clients ready to consider starting.

## Key questions answered
- How do I begin?
- What is typical session length?
- Online how?
- What is not on this page (fees)?

## Required Fact IDs
PRO-012, PRO-014, PRO-011, BUS-005, BUS-009, BUS-013, PRO-005

## Permitted claims
Online sessions; ~45 minutes; contact via Email/Telegram channel types; process steps in plain language; fees not listed.

## Prohibited claims
SAFE-005, SAFE-001, SAFE-009; invented cancellation rules; published prices.

## Content structure
1. Short intro  
2. Step 1: Contact (Email / Telegram)  
3. Step 2: First conversation / session suitability (non-clinical, no invasive asks)  
4. Step 3: Ongoing online sessions (45 minutes)  
5. Quiet note: fees not published on the site  
6. CTA Contact  

## CTA
Primary: Contact · Secondary: Services

## Internal links
→ Contact, Services · ← Home

## SEO / GEO
Practical Q&A extractability; no fee/local SEO invention.

## Bilingual parity
Same steps; EN notes Persian therapy language.

## Acceptance
Short page; no admin bloat; no fee table.
